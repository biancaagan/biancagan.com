BLT's Garden

A raised garden bed with an automated watering system using an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi and MQTT protocol.

The main purpose for this (besides just having a nice little raised garden in our backyard), was to be able to not worry about our garden not getting water when we are both out of town.

That being said, as of late Summer 2026, we have been moving stuff around in our garden, reorganizing and planting more things, and since one of us has always been home lately, the remote watering system has been put on pause. There's something calm and routine about going outside first thing in the morning with a cup of coffee and watering your plants manually...

Component List:

  • Raised garden bed kit from Home Depot
  • Arduino UNO R4 WiFi
  • 12V submersible water pump + added in-line filter
  • 5V relay
  • 1/2" tubing for the mainline
  • 1/4" tubing for jumps from the mainline to the micro bubblers/sprayers
  • Sparkfun soil moisture sensors

The Arduino controls the water pump, and I originally had it set to water at 9:00AM pacific time. I used NTP (network time protocol) to control the clock. It ended up watering about 15 minutes earlier (at 8:45AM). I originally had (3) Sparkfun moisture sensors that were going to the (2) tomato plants and the jalapeño plant... they aren't properly mapped, and overall just aren't the most accurate reading.

I am also sending data to my Shiftr.io broker like this:

"{\"state\":\"" + stateVal +
"\",\"moisture1\":" + String(map(analogRead(soil1Pin), 350, 880, 0, 100)) +
",\"moisture2\":" + String(map(analogRead(soil2Pin), 350, 880, 0, 100)) +
",\"moisture3\":" + String(map(analogRead(soil3Pin), 350, 880, 0, 100)) + "}";

On the webpage side, I use javascript to parse out the data and display them in the browser. You can visit the webpage here. It will also keep a brief recent history as long as the page isn't refreshed.

The webpage also has the option to manually water for x amount of seconds. Anyone with the password can send a message to the broker which gets read by Arduino to specify a specific amount of seconds to water, outside of the preset watering times.
