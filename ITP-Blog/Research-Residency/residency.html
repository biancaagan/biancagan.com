ITP/IMA Research Residency (2023 - 2024)

After graduating from ITP, I was lucky to be selected as one of the
P-Comp (Physical Computation) residents.

Analog Synth (Residency Project)

I have occassionally built simple noise makers since taking Billy Bennett's Hacking Audio Hardware class during my first semester at ITP.
During my research residency I experimented with many different analog audio circuits such as various oscillators, filters, mixers, etc. For the resident show I decided to continue this exploration of analog audio and built my own version of a modular synthesizer.

Drawing Bugs

I wanted to continue building other 'drawing machines' and wanted to go a bit more analog.

Binary Clock Google Chrome Extension

Had some office hours with someone who had questions regarding their project for the class Time.
It inspired me to make a clock.

Mini ADM (Abstract Drawing Machine)

Expanding on my thesis, I built a mini version of ADM (Abstract Drawing Machine) to hang on the wall in front of my desk.
I've been playing around with different equations and using the Desmos online graphing calculator.
