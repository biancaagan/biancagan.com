Drawing Bugs

by Bianca Gan, December 2023 (ongoing)

I wanted to continue building other 'drawing machines' and wanted to go a bit more analog.

As a first iteration, I took some lengths of brass wire, a tiny vibrating motor, and a cell coin battery, and turned a marker and a pen into little drawing bugs that buzz around on a piece of paper leaving marks in their trail.

The friction between the marker/pen tip and paper needs to be fiddled around with the angles of the legs.
