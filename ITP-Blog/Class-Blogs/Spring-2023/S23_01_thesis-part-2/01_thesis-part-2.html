Thesis: ADM

My thesis — ADM (Abstract Drawing Machine) — is a drawing machine with multiple functions that allow it to be both an art installation as well as an interactive machine that encourages people to be curious and play. This project is both the machine itself as well as the process of research and experimentation, a custom Arduino library, and many many drawings.

It is a vertical XY plotter that uses a dual stepper motor-controlled pulley system to drag a drawing utensil across a surface.

The idea of ADM stemmed from something I mentioned in my entry statement nearly two years ago – this idea of an interactive visualization where anyone can create their own piece of art. ADM is a self-indulgent project that not only focused on the end product of a functioning drawing machine, but the journey of building, experimenting, and playing that has (so far) taken place throughout my 2nd year at ITP.

ADM's two current interactive functions are – (1) inviting users to leave a mark on the canvas by guiding ADM to draw out words, and (2) encouraging people to explore the follow-me mode that tracks a single person’s path directly in front of the canvas. When play-testing one of my very firrst projects here at ITP, I witnessed a fun moment of my classmates playing with my Arduino-driven version of making music with spoons and glasses of water – their reactions of excitement and joy when discovering and playing with this piece were something I held dear throughout my time at ITP, and it is something I strove to make a part of my thesis project.

Through ADM, I want people to be curious, play, discover, and enjoy watching this little robot make some art.
