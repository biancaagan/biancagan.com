Week 03 - More Modeling

For this modeling exercise we worked off of these reference images of an Ikea plant stand:

At first I started off by drawing from the top view and revolving that into a circle pattern to make the three plates, then extruding from there.

However, when I started to think about adding any details like fillets and such, I realized maybe an easier approach would be to draw just one side, extrude of the pieces, add all the details, then create the circle pattern after the solids are made.
