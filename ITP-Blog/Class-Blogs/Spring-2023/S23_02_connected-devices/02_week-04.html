Week 04 - Device Data Dashboard (cont'd)

I’ve started to make some visual updates to the dashboard taking into account some feedback from last class. I’m trying to incorporate more data visually and have made a calendar in the style of the Ring-A-Date calendar by designer Giorgio Della Beffa.

Here is my github repo. And you can visit the site here.

Eventually, I’d like each date to have a ring around it displaying my thesis activity, similar to that of the Apple watch activity ring (thanks Julia for the idea).

Kind of like this, the ring would total into 24 segments for the 24 hours in a day, and would update accordingly throughout the day–

As of now, I just have the ring around the current date changing between red and green when my device is on and sending to MQTT.
